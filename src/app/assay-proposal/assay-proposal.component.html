Requirements

Proposals are reviewed case by case. Partner screens should be:

  • Relevant to global health
  • Different from prior or ongoing screens, e.g. species, strain or isolate, lifecycle stage, or readout
  • Miniaturized: under 50 µL (384- or 1536-well), suited to a single-point screen with under 1% hits to follow up
  • Covered by an MTA that ensures global access to the data, published on REFRAMEdb after 12 months

Compound supply

About 0.5 nmol of each compound for the primary screen, and up to 4 nmol per hit across the campaign.
