Primary screen plates

Shipped 6 May 2026

Calibr dry-spotted the collection onto assay-ready 384-well plates in singlicate, as set out in your plate layout. Compounds are blinded; your compound key maps each well to an RFM ID.

Download compound key

Why pre-spotted plates? The collection is finite and meant for many partners, so each partner gets nanomole amounts: about 0.5 nmol of each compound for the primary screen, and up to 4 nmol for hits across the whole screen.

Dose-response plates

After your hit list. Calibr will email you when they ship.

Calibr prepares two replicate sets of dose-response plates from your hits: 8-point, 1:3 serial dilutions starting at 10 µM unless agreed otherwise. You can use one set for a counter screen if you add a counter screen to this record.
