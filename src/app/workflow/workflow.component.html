Stage 1

Before screening begins

  1. Partner
    Fill out the Assay Proposal form
  2. Both
    MTA template provided by Calibr for signature at both institutions
  3. Partner
    Final Assay Description form delivered to Calibr (reframescreens@scripps.edu); pre-screen meeting scheduled
  4. Partner
    By default, partner agrees to a standard primary screen (singlicate) and two dose response sets for initial follow-up on.

    Dose sets are provided as replicate plates. Plates are prepared as 8-point 1:3 serial dilutions, starting at 10 μM unless otherwise discussed. Exceptions must be approved by the ReFRAME committee. For these, the partner must complete the Quantity Exception form. More information on standard usage can be found here.

  5. Partner
    Optional: Partner sends Calibr controls dissolved in DMSO. Active concentration adjusted to match volume of the screening compounds whenever possible. On average, 100 µL of 10 mM stock is sufficient.

Stage 2

Screening round 1

  1. Calibr
    Calibr sends partner pre-spotted plates and compound keys (as blinded CBR IDs) for primary screen.
  2. Partner
    Partner performs screen and selects hits (up to 1% of the library)
  3. Partner
    Partner sends hitlist as CBR IDs using the Primary Data form
  4. Calibr
    Calibr sends partner two sets of pre-spotted dose response plates. One set can be used for a counter screen upon submission of an additional assay description form for the counter screen.
  5. Partner
    Collaborator sends Calibr dose response data via Dose Response Data form
  6. Calibr
    Calibr sends collaborator structure and identities of confirmed hits

Stage 3 · On ReFRAME committee approval

Additional counter screens

  1. Calibr
    In some cases, additional material for counter screen assays can be provided by Calibr.

    Requests for additional material or other resources will be reviewed by the ReFRAME committee. Partners are asked to complete Screening Round 1 before additional requests for compounds.

Stage 4

After screening

  1. Calibr
    Dose response data uploaded to ReFRAMEdb.org by Calibr 1 year after partner receives structure identities.

    Partner can contact Calibr to request a delay under some circumstances.

  2. Partner
    Public success is the best way for our partners to support the ReFRAME project.

    Please notify Calibr of any publications, grants or studies that resulted from the ReFRAME screen using the Milestone document.
